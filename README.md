# 📊 Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize business sales performance.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of **Sales, Profit, Quantity, Products, Categories, Regions, and Monthly Performance** to help understand business trends and identify key performance areas.

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track total profit and quantity sold
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Analyze category-wise sales contribution
- Understand monthly sales and profit trends
- Provide an interactive and easy-to-understand business report

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Total Sales by Product
Shows the quantity/performance of different products such as:
- Shoes
- Mouse
- Desk
- Headphones
- Monitor
- T-Shirt
- Water Bottle
- Office Chair
- Notebook
- Mobile
- Laptop
- Keyboard
- Jeans
- Backpack
- Pen Set

### 2. Sales & Profit by Region
The dashboard compares performance across:
- Central
- East
- North
- South
- West

### 3. Sales by Category
Category contribution is visualized using a donut chart.
Major categories include:
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Fashion
- Bags
- Accessories
- Stationery

### 4. Monthly Sales & Profit
Monthly performance is analyzed from **January to December**, helping identify high and low-performing months.

### 5. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes slicers for:
- **Month**
- **Region**

These filters make the dashboard interactive and allow users to analyze specific segments.

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Electronics** is the largest sales category, contributing approximately **73%** of total sales.
- **October** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **₹5.7M**.
- **Central Region** is the strongest-performing region based on sales.
- **Shoes** has the highest quantity among the displayed products.
- The dashboard provides a quick view of overall business performance through KPI cards.
